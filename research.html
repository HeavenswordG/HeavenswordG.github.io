Research

Observing environmental change from above

I develop scalable ways to turn Earth-observation data into evidence about changing forests, landscapes, and cities. My work brings together ecological questions and geospatial computation across pixels, point clouds, and time.

01

Forest disturbance & recovery

Measuring how wildfire, prior burns, and climate shape forest structure, post-disturbance recovery, and the risk of long-term forest loss.

Time series · LiDAR · Ecological modeling
02

Satellite photogrammetry

Extracting reliable 3D information from stereo and multi-view satellite imagery for buildings, glaciers, and other dynamic surfaces.

3D reconstruction · DSMs · Change detection
03

Geospatial AI

Designing machine-learning methods that connect multimodal observations with interpretable, decision-relevant information at landscape scale.

Deep learning · Computer vision · Data fusion
